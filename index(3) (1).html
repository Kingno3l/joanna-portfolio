ABOUT JOANNA

From early childhood teaching to inclusive educational innovation.

HER STORY

A career shaped by a simple question: how can every child learn and grow?

Joanna's professional journey began in early childhood education and developed through classroom teaching, language education, career counseling, educational consulting, research, and educational leadership.

Early foundationRaised in Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria, with roots in Edo State. Her first teaching experience was as a kindergarten teacher, where her interest in preschool and early childhood education began.
Language & teachingAfter earning a B.A. in Linguistics from the University of Ibadan, she continued in education, including teaching English Language, Grammar, and Civic Education during NYSC in Imo State.
Seeing different needsCareer counseling and educational consulting exposed her to learners with different goals, learning needs, language backgrounds, and educational challenges.
Exceptional RootsThose experiences helped inspire Exceptional Roots Learning, a parent-assisted educational technology platform designed to support exceptional and diverse learners through structured learning opportunities at home.
Leadership & researchShe pursued graduate study in education at Carolina University (Master of Education) to strengthen her ability to understand systems, improve educational practice, and better serve learners and families.
TodayShe currently teaches second grade in Winston-Salem, applying instructional, literacy, assessment, and inclusive practices directly in the classroom while continuing her research and educational innovation work.

FOUNDED 2023 · EDUCATIONAL INNOVATION

Exceptional Roots Learning ↗

Exceptional Roots Learning is the clearest expression of Joanna's long-standing interest in early childhood education, diverse learning needs, family support, and accessible learning technology.

It is a parent-guided educational technology platform designed to support preschool and elementary learners through predictable routines, low-stimulation learning experiences, visual supports, retrieval activities, milestone tracking, and parent participation.
